# Product

## Register

product

## Users

U.S. community members who need to identify their federal and state-legislative representatives, understand the limits of the available evidence, and confidently contact the appropriate office.

## Product Purpose

Turn a residential address into trustworthy, clearly scoped civic information and practical contact actions. Success means users can distinguish live identity data from fictional demonstrations, understand what each office does, inspect literal source-backed records without inferred political scoring, and take an informed next step.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, civic, direct. The interface should feel calm and authoritative without becoming institutional or partisan.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic political scorecards, partisan campaign styling, unsupported summaries, decorative dashboard clutter, generic card grids, and interactions that obscure ordinary actions behind novel controls.

## Design Principles

- Make provenance and coverage boundaries visible at the point of use.
- Prefer clear civic language and familiar controls over clever presentation.
- Keep the address-to-official-to-action path short and predictable.
- Use government-level color as orientation, never as a political judgment.
- Preserve user trust through explicit privacy, demo, loading, empty, and error states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic announcements, 44px touch targets, user-selectable text sizes and color themes, sufficient contrast, and reduced motion.
